The current site

Where estatelinks.net lets the firm down

Everything below can be checked on the live site today. Each card says what is wrong, why it matters and how the new site handles it.

Broken

Main buttons leave the website

“Get A Quote”, “Explore More”, the footer “Home” and “About us” all point to a third-party test address, not estatelinks.net. If that address goes offline, the buttons stop working.

Get A Quote → estatelink.[developer test domain]/get-a-quote/ every link stays on the firm's own domain.
Credibility

Placeholder text in the testimonials

The “Words from our Clients” section shows dummy Latin text, repeated three times and credited to the same made-up name.

“Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet…” · Faridat Vishnu (×3) removed until the firm has real client quotes to show.
Credibility

The logo is a screenshot of a PDF

The logo file is a browser capture of a PDF, so it looks soft and pixelated on modern screens.

screencapture-file-C-Users-user-Downloads-estatelinks-logo-2018-master-pdf… a clean logo file, with a version for dark backgrounds.
Broken

Links that go nowhere

The Facebook, YouTube, Twitter and LinkedIn icons and the “Learn more” button link to nothing. The “Terms & Conditions” link on the sign-up form opens an About page.

every button leads somewhere useful.
Missed business

No way to ask for a valuation

Valuation is the firm's core service, but the site has no form for it. A bank or family that wants a valuation has to find a phone number and call.

a 3-step request form for each service, sent straight to the team's inbox.
Credibility

Listings look unfinished

Agents show as an email address, some listings say “Price on call” while the description gives the price, and bedroom, bathroom and size details are often missing or contradict the title.

a consistent listing layout with photo gallery, specs, WhatsApp, viewing and video-tour buttons.
Missed business

No rentals, no costs upfront

Tenants in Lagos want to know the full move-in cost before they visit. The site has no rental listings and no way to show agency, legal, service charge or diesel costs.

“Total to move in” on every rental, with a full breakdown.
Credibility

Dated content

The Northern England investment page relies on 2021–2023 figures, and project pages still show delivery dates that have now passed.

staff can update any of it in minutes from the admin area.
Running cost

Every change needs a developer, and it renews every year

The site runs on WordPress with the Elementor page builder: heavy on mobile data, dependent on plugins, and tied to yearly hosting and licence renewals.

a fast single-page site with a simple admin area, on free hosting.
The new site

What I have built

Your content, in your brand colours, rebuilt around the things visitors want to do: rent, buy, value, manage, invest and build.

Homepage

It opens with an action

The headline cycles through “Rent with us”, “Value with us”, “Manage with us” and four more. Each one has its own photo and a button straight to that service.

Rentals

The real cost, before the visit

  • Total to move in, with rent, agency, legal, service charge and diesel itemised
  • Book a viewing or request a video tour in one tap
  • WhatsApp enquiry on every listing
  • Prices in naira, dollars or pounds
For investors

What would this property earn?

Every property for sale in Nigeria shows an earnings estimate: rent a year, net yield and years to earn back the price, with a 10-year chart. A buy-to-let calculator covers the UK homes.

Enquiries

Questions that fit the service

Pick a valuation and the form asks for the property, its purpose and its title document. Pick property management and it asks for the number of units and whether the building is let. Every request lands in the admin inbox.

Admin

Staff run the site themselves

  • Dashboard with new enquiries and listings that need attention
  • Add, edit, reorder and feature listings
  • Upload, caption and reorder photos
  • Edit the team, projects, FAQs, homepage text and exchange rates
  • Mark enquiries as contacted or closed, and reply on WhatsApp
Mobile

Built for the phone first

Most visitors in Lagos arrive on a phone, often on mobile data. The new site is one light page that loads fast and works with one thumb.
